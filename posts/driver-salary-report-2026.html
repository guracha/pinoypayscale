Market Evolution Summary

In 2026, the Philippine driver market has transitioned from general labor to a specialized service role. Employers now prioritize safety certifications, digital literacy (for GPS and telematics), and seniority over basic driving skills. This report breaks down the complex factors influencing take-home pay.
