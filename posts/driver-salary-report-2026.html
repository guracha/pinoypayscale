Labor Demand & Regional Disparities

Demand for drivers remains robust, particularly in logistics and e-commerce. Metro Manila (NCR) continues to be the epicenter of demand, offering higher wages but also contending with intense traffic and higher living costs. Regional hubs like Cebu and Davao offer competitive salaries with a more relaxed work environment, making them increasingly attractive alternatives.
